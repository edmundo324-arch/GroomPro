# Employee compensation

Manage compensation inside the shared employee editor. Leaving employee rules disabled retains legacy service percentages. Service exemptions always override the employee's earnings. Percentage rules use the discounted line amount; flat per-dog rules count a dog once per matching rule on the ticket. Package percentages apply to the allocated component prices so the package is not commissioned multiple times at its full price.

The editor opens from Schedule by selecting an employee's name. Employee Details contains protected contact, address, employment and emergency-contact records. Pay & Commissions exposes the existing rates and rules directly, with its own Save Compensation action. Booking & Access retains qualifications, location, PIN, family-arrival limits and the complete online-booking class catalog. Unsaved pay edits survive switching these tabs.

Selected private profiles use a manager-authorized endpoint; ordinary employee directories do not include these details. Supplemental HR fields are stored under tenant-scoped `EMPLOYEE_HR:<employeeId>` settings and excluded from general business settings responses. Saves include only changed private fields, preserving imported values and unrelated settings.

Qualification uses the last 30 calendar dates, inclusive of today, in the employee's location timezone. Completed grooming assignments count distinct dogs per worked date. Their service/check-in date establishes a worked date. Managers record any other actual worked dates, including days with zero dogs. A scheduled shift alone is not evidence that the employee worked. Hire date controls minimum-tenure thresholds; an unknown hire date cannot qualify a tenure requirement.

Rates change automatically by default. If the tenant enables manager approval, the last approved rate (or base rate before the first approval) remains in use until a manager approves the new qualified rate. Closing a paid ticket freezes its line earnings. Later rule edits do not rewrite closed tickets.

Hourly rates and tip participation are compensation configuration; this change does not create a time-clock/payroll payment processor. No paychecks or transfers are initiated.

## In-house payroll preparation

The agreed first jurisdiction is United States, Texas. Gusto is not being integrated. Each business chooses between individual employee pay methods (factory default) and one shared pay method. These setup choices must not rewrite ticket commissions or historical earnings. Unselected methods remain unselected; a salary choice is not an overtime exemption or a salary amount.

Existing Schedule → Time Clock provides clock-in/out, breaks and audited corrections. Attendance reports do not determine whether recorded breaks are paid, establish an overtime exemption or substitute for a reviewed payroll calculation. Payroll remains unfinished until wage calculations, withholding and year-to-date balances, pay-run review and approval, bank transmission and tax filing acknowledgments are implemented and validated.

Prosperity's [Treasury Management service](https://www.prosperitybankusa.com/treasury-management/) supports payroll direct deposits through ACH Origination. Its [Treasury Center payments guide](https://www.prosperitybankusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Treasury-Center-Payments-Training-Guide-Oct-2024.pdf) documents file import. The user's bank enrollment is not yet confirmed. The bank must provide enrollment confirmation, origin/company identifiers, accepted file/offset requirements, approval and funding rules, limits, cutoffs and fees. An automated server connection has not been confirmed. GroomPro sending for multiple employers also requires an approved arrangement, distinct from an employer submitting its own payroll; Prosperity publishes a [Third-Party Sender agreement](https://www.prosperitybankusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ACH-3rd-Party-Sender.pdf).

Direct employment-tax filing is a separate integration. The [IRS MeF requirements](https://www.irs.gov/e-file-providers/modernized-e-file-mef-for-employment-taxes) require applicable provider registration and software assurance testing. Bank support does not establish IRS or Texas filing authorization. No live payments, agency registrations, tax filings or bank-account changes have been made by this preparation.

VIP first-recurring-payment rules use service categories from the enrolled plan. The activation line identifies the selling employee and can be reassigned in POS. `recordFirstBillingCommission` is the idempotent payment-confirmation hook: it accepts only a recorded PAID/SUCCEEDED recurring payment with a processing timestamp, excludes the $1 activation, locks the membership, and stores one earning per membership. The existing application has no recurring payment processor; its future tenant-specific processor must call this hook inside the verified payment transaction. Pending scheduled payments never count as earnings. No actual membership charge was initiated during development.
